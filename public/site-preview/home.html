03 · How I work

Principles I keep returning to.

I'd rather ship a smaller result I can defend than a bigger one that quietly leaks. Every project below is written with a reviewer looking over my shoulder.

Principle / 01

Integrity before performance

A clean split, a reproducible seed, and a documented feature list come before any model choice. If a number isn't reproducible, it isn't a number.

Principle / 02

Explainability is the deliverable

The stakeholder isn't buying AUROC; they're buying trust. SHAP, calibration, and plain-language summaries are part of the model, not a wrapper.

Principle / 03

Small is honest

With 299 patients or 229 trials, the right answer is often a short list of well-supported claims — not a long list of weakly-supported ones.
