Interactive Card Sorting Analysis.

A live behavioural movement viewer for card-sorting trials. The project studies how participants organise Kings, Queens, Jacks, and blank cards on an 8x8 board, then turns the movement path into evidence about strategy, messiness, retry progression, and success recovery.
